## Summary

### Independent Filmmaking Philosophy

- No single monolithic approach to filmmaking exists
- Three types of filmmakers:
  - Pure entertainment focus
  - Pure artistic/commentary focus
  - Hybrid approach combining both
- Teaching advantage: real experience vs. theory
  - 30 years of actual filmmaking provides validity
  - Examples from completed projects, not hypotheticals
- Artistic commitment is sacred
  - Requires blood, sweat, tears
  - Timing and luck create unique opportunities
  - Responsibility to comment on world events through work

### Film Grammar and Director’s Tools

- Essential filmmaker skills to master:
  1. Scriptwriting and storytelling
  2. Visual direction - translating words to visual life
  3. Music as directorial tool
  4. Film grammar fundamentals
- Can start projects without completed script
- Continuous learning mindset required even after decades

### Pre-Production Research Process

- Story selection based on persistent thinking
  - Multiple story ideas compete for attention
  - The one you keep returning to becomes the chosen project
- Extensive research phase before writing:
  - Fill multiple notebooks with dialogue, plot points, ideas
  - Transfer everything to 5x7 index cards
  - Organize cards by category (dialogue, plot, scenes)
  - Arrange cards chronologically from first to last scene
- Card system advantages:
  - Easy scene reordering when structure doesn’t work
  - Dialogue can be moved between scenes
  - Flexible organization allows experimentation
- Script writing begins only after filling entire box of organized cards
