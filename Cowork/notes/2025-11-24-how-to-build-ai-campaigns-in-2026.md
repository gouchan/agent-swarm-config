### AI Creative Industry Evolution Timeline

- **2023 Foundation Phase**: Visually impressive but professionally challenging period 
  - First Midjourney iteration produced “cool artistic elements” but obvious AI aesthetic markers
  - Technical barriers: consistency nearly impossible to achieve at campaign scale
  - Workflow complexity: required custom training, custom Loras, highly technical expertise
  - Production reality: generated thousands of images to curate handful of usable campaign assets
  - Post-production burden: significant Photoshop work required to fix AI artifacts and inconsistencies
  - Integration challenges: real campaign production remained largely impractical for most creatives
  - Market perception: clear “you can tell it was made with AI” quality ceiling
- **2024 Technical Advancement Phase**: Major evolution for skilled practitioners willing to invest in R&D 
  - **Config UI workflow capabilities**: Enabled impressive product integration results for technical users
  - **Custom training advances**: Multiple-shot storytelling became feasible with proper setup
  - **Team requirements**: Workflow engineering became critical competitive advantage (presenter employs Pablo as “workflow engineer wizard”)
  - **Tool ecosystem complexity**: Multiple platforms required 
    - Midjourney/Reve for initial ideation and mood boarding
    - Config UI for actual production and campaign execution
    - Extensive R&D time investment for consistent results
  - **Accessibility limitation**: “Pretty limiting in terms of who can have access” to professional-grade results
  - **Behind-scenes reality**: Year and a half ago, consistent story with multiple shots required thousands of image generations plus extensive post-production curation
  - **Market positioning**: Most practitioners still couldn’t achieve consistent production quality despite impressive individual results
- **2025 Accessibility Revolution** (particularly final 3 months): Technical barrier elimination period 
  - **Model breakthrough**: Nano Banana Pro, C Dream, Seed Dream democratized access
  - **Resolution advancement**: Native high-resolution output eliminates Magnify, upscaling tool dependencies
  - **Product integration mastery**: Achieves near-perfect success rates using refined workflow systems
  - **Creative control expansion**: Full environment, vibe, product control without traditional production overhead constraints
  - **Video quality leap**: Cling and similar models producing “no artifacts anymore” broadcast-standard content
  - **Workflow evolution**: Still utilize Config UI for maximum consistency but accessibility “definitely way more accessible than before”
  - **Story capability**: Full narrative construction with multiple shots, eliminating previous technical storytelling limitations
  - **Professional threshold**: Presenter’s opinion - “for still images product integration, we reached a point where there is no reason for not doing a photo shoot with AI”

- **World building foundation process** (comprehensive pre-production phase):
