## Summary

### Application Requirements

- Submit 500-word letter explaining who you are and why you deserve a place
  - Creative writing task, not resume or CV format
  - Demonstrate ability to write persuasively and get attention
  - Show you can take something (yourself) and sell it effectively
  - Don’t use boring prose listing schools and recent work history
  - Distill all writing/copywriting knowledge into one compelling piece
  - This is essence of copywriting: making reader interested and excited to learn more
- Optional: Include writing sample (max 2 pages)
  - Submit if you already write financial copy or other copy
  - Focus on headline and lead sections for copy samples
  - Skip entirely if no relevant writing experience
  - 500-word letter reviewed first - sample only viewed if letter impresses
  - Background in copywriting helpful but doesn’t guarantee translation to financial copy

### Selection Criteria & Philosophy

- Very limited spots due to significant 1-on-1 coaching and mentoring commitment
  - Expecting high volume of applicants
  - Individual feedback and guidance requires small cohort
- Experience not essential for acceptance
  - Many top industry professionals started with no background
  - Instructor started in 2009 with minimal advertising/direct response experience
  - Will accept people with zero finance or copywriting background
- Looking for natural talent and potential over credentials
  - Seeking “spark,” creativity, or something that sets applicants apart
  - Want to see quality of thinking and writing ability
  - Not interested in degrees, schools, or formal experience
  - Care about what experience gives you as writer, not credentials themselves

### Application Strategy & Honesty

- Be completely truthful about experience level
  - If you have 20 years financial copywriting experience, say so
  - If you don’t have experience, don’t pretend or fabricate
  - Don’t falsely claim training from industry figures
  - Financial copywriting world is small - most senior people know each other
  - Easy to verify claims quickly at copy chief/publisher level
  - Lies become red flags and collapse eventually in career
- Build honest foundation for relationship from start
- Focus on demonstrating actual writing ability rather than invented credentials

### Standing Out & Creativity

- Expect 1,000+ applications competing for attention
- Think like copywriter facing crowded marketplace
  - Consider what others will likely submit
  - Apply same principles used in marketing: cut through noise
  - Speak to reader (instructor) in way that makes them listen
- Challenge: make your piece stand out among many applications
- Use creativity to surprise and engage reader
- Remember this mirrors real copywriting work of getting prospect attention

### Submission Process & Timeline

- Email address provided directly below video
- Deadline clearly stated below video
- Contact same email with questions if anything unclear
- Take time to craft quality application - don’t rush submission
  - Multiple drafts encouraged before sending
  - Effort and consideration will show in final application
- Strict single submission policy
  - No revised versions accepted
  - Don’t send initial draft then improved versions 24 hours later
  - Multiple submissions seen as red flag
- Review process begins after deadline closes
  - Places not allocated until after deadline
  - Time needed to review all applications thoroughly
  - All applicants contacted shortly after deadline with updates on progress

### Program Mission & Structure

- Completely free program with no charges for advice or training
- Could sell similar program for significant money but choosing not to
- Sole goal: find and develop best copywriting talent for Market Wise business
- Mission: turn good writers into exceptional financial copywriters
- Focus on writers with potential and develop that potential fully
- No ulterior motives beyond talent identification and development
- Significant investment in individual coaching and mentoring for accepted participants
