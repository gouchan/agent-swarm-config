### Moment-Led vs Product-Led Strategy

- Product-led: builds from features/attributes outward
  - Examples: GoPro (camera), Dyson (innovation), Intel (chip technology)
  - Inside-out approach starting with product attributes
- Moment-led: starts with consumption moments, builds strategy from there
  - Examples: Instagram (social sharing), IKEA (home living moments), iPhone (creativity/photo moments)
  - Outside-in approach from moment of use
- Julian’s 4-step moment-led process:
  - Identify the moment brand is playing in
  - Define goal/aspiration of that moment
  - Identify what’s standing in the way of that goal
  - Position how brand can be part of the solution

### Graza Case Study Breakdown

- Moment identification: Cooking (Gen Z target)
- Consumer goals using 24 consumer goals cheat sheet:
  - Creativity and self-expression through cooking
  - Fun, approachable, joyful cooking experience
  - Confidence in kitchen abilities
- Benefit ladder construction:
  - Product features: 100% single origin olives, two simple ingredients, fun packaging
  - Functional benefits: Easier cooking, squeeze control, no mess, no intimidation
  - Clear guidance: “Drizzle” vs “Sizzle” usage, cheap for everyday use
- Barriers preventing Gen Z from cooking:
  - Fear of failure and performance anxiety
  - Cooking feels like intimidating performance test vs joyful activity
  - Convenience of Uber Eats/delivery apps too easy
  - Lack of time and skills
  - Pressure for results to be “postable” on social media
  - Disconnection from cooking culture
- Brand strategy solution: “Made to be used”
  - Make cooking accessible and fun for daily Gen Z use
  - Transform olive oil from trophy-like glass bottles to approachable tools
  - Playful advertising showing easy applications (potato chips, not high-concept chef food)
- Glass bottle expansion rationale:
  - Still fits moment-led strategy if goal is more Gen Z cooking
  - Addresses recycling concerns and single-use plastic issues
  - Strategy bigger than bottle format if it helps achieve cooking goal
