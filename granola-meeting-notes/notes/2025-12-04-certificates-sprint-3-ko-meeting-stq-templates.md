### Promo Template Refresh (Section 1)

- Problem statement: Current templates showing fatigue with repetitive “same info, same way” presentation despite strong spend performance
- Scope: Redesign existing high-performing template structures while maintaining core effectiveness
- Four proven template categories for variation enhancement:
  - Text-only format: Large offer text with minimal visual elements (current top performer)
  - Wrapper design: Image positioned below with copy placement above offer
  - Quote templates: Customer testimonials underneath with hero image on top
  - Button format: Offer display along bottom integrated with existing imagery and messaging
- Design parameters: Focus on layout diversity, color variations, font sizing/shaping, compositional changes
- Technical requirements:
  - All templates must support three aspect ratios (1:1, 16:9, 9:16) for Meta platform deployment
  - StoryTech integration compatibility for rapid ad assembly and testing
  - Templates designed as separate system from new static assets but must accommodate both legacy and new creative
- Constraint: Templates require no partner review (design-only changes), enabling faster deployment

### Message Testing Strategy (Section 2)

- Research foundation: Recent user interviews with actual Certs customers identifying two critical decision-making factors
- Creative development targeting 16-24 new static assets across two insight-driven themes
- Asset distribution: 8-12 pieces per theme, flexible based on production timeline and creative strength

#### Theme 1: Course Value Proposition

- User insight: “Why is this course more valuable than individual core classes?”
- Pain point: Customers questioning ROI on $300+ course investment versus free/cheaper alternatives
- Messaging focus areas:
  - In-depth content depth and quality differentiation
  - Assessment and capstone project value-adds
  - Comprehensive learning experience beyond basic instruction
- Creative direction: Communicate tangible benefits and outcomes justifying premium pricing
- Success metrics: Address budget/timeline concerns while reinforcing educational depth

#### Theme 2: Content Relevance and Immediate Applicability

- User insight: Strong correlation between perceived applicability and course quality ratings
- Proven messaging patterns from high-performing ads:
  - Navy SEAL course: “Calm is a skill, train for it” (consistent top performer across campaigns)
  - Moonshot course: “Prototype in hours not months” (sustained high performance)
- Creative tension principle: Both successful messages invert common expectations
  - Calm traditionally viewed as natural trait, not trainable skill
  - Prototyping typically associated with lengthy development cycles
- Messaging strategy: Identify specific, grabbable takeaways customers can immediately implement
- Creative execution: Focus on “learn today, apply tomorrow” positioning with concrete skill applications

- Moonshot course demographics: 25-55 age range, senior-level professionals requiring sophisticated positioning
- Navy SEAL course: Broader age spectrum appeal, leadership themes resonate across career stages
- Format recommendations: Bold text-only treatments given previous high-performance data
