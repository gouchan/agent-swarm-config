## Summary

### Setting BFCM List Growth Goals

- BFCM market opportunity and baseline setting

  - **Key Stats**: $10.8B Black Friday 2024 sales (up 10% YoY), $13.3B Cyber Monday 2024
  - Core principle: More profiles = more marketing opportunities = bigger BFCM revenue slice
  - **Critical insight**: 47% of BFCM sales come from existing customers - strategy must capture the other 53%

- Accessing List Growth Report in Klaviyo

  - Navigation: Audience > Lists & Segments > Select main email list > List Growth tab
  - Date range setup: Q4 2024 (October 1 - December 31, 2024) or last 3 months for new brands
  - View options: Weekly or monthly granularity available

- Understanding growth metrics

  - **Total Members Graph**: Shows cumulative subscriber count over selected period
  - **Net Membership Graph** (more important): Green = gains, red = losses, top number = net change
  - Example interpretation: October +6/-2, November +3/-8, December +4/-1
  - Subscriber Sources section: Shows acquisition channels (multi-step forms, Shopify, etc.)
  - Unsubscribe Methods: Tracks churn by method (unsubscribe page, hard bounces, manual)

- Goal-setting framework

  - Use historical data + current year site traffic changes to set realistic targets
  - Focus on acquiring customers similar to existing best customers for long-term retention
  - Start early to allow time for targeted strategy development vs. broad acquisition

- Traffic source analysis requirements

  - Evaluate all current channels: social media, SEM, display ads, referrals
  - **Tools for analysis**:
    - Ecommerce platform built-in analytics
    - Google Analytics for deeper traffic insights
  - **ROAS calculation**: (Ad Revenue ÷ Ad Spend) × 100
  - Prioritize channels with highest signup rates and purchase conversions
  - Investment strategy: Focus limited time/resources on highest-performing channels

### Optimizing the Onsite Experience

- Performance baseline establishment

  - Review signup form analytics for all existing forms (embed, popup, flyout)
  - Check popup form benchmarks to compare against similar businesses
  - Performance tiers: Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent - identify improvement opportunities

- **Email & SMS Multi-Step Form (Top Priority Implementation)**

  - **Why multi-step**: Collects both channels without overwhelming visitors
  - **Step 1 setup**: Email collection + first name for personalization + strong offer
    - Example offer structure: “Get 10% off when you join our email list”
    - First name enables immediate personalization in welcome messages
  - **Step 2 configuration**: SMS opt-in with layered incentive
    - Example: If 10% for email, offer additional 5% for SMS (total 15%)
    - Position SMS as “optional but valuable”
  - **Step 3 execution**: Confirmation directing to email/subscription verification
  - **Targeting rule**: Show only to new visitors, not existing Klaviyo profiles

- Advanced form optimization tactics

  - **Form Teaser implementation**: Allows visitors to reopen forms after initial dismissal
    - Addresses common user behavior: initial hesitation, then desire to return after browsing
    - Example display: “Get 10% off” teaser button remains visible
  - **Exit Intent Forms**: Trigger when mouse moves toward URL bar or back button
    - Less intrusive than immediate popups
    - Example messaging: “Wait! Get 10% off your order - sign up now and redeem within 7 days”
    - Perfect for A/B testing different exit offers

- Specialized form types

  - **SMS Smart Opt-In Form**: Auto-fills phone numbers on iOS/Android devices
    - Reduces friction by eliminating manual phone entry
    - Minimizes distractions - users stay on site during opt-in
    - **Requirement**: Must be on paid SMS plan to access
  - **Banner Forms**: Remind visitors of current offers or highlight new products
    - Less intrusive than popups, maintain constant visibility

- Form optimization checklist and best practices

  - **Minimum setup**: 3 forms with different targeting
    - Embed form for general use
    - Multi-step form for new visitors
    - SMS-only form for existing email subscribers
  - **Technical settings**:
    - Add teasers to all popup forms
    - Configure form collision avoidance (Settings > Don’t show multiple forms simultaneously)
    - Set time delays or scroll percentages to reduce “rage clicks”
  - **A/B testing strategy**: Test timing, offers, design elements
  - **Preview requirement**: Always preview forms on mobile devices before publishing

### Acquiring Valuable Leads with Lookalike Audiences

- Platform integration setup

  - **Available integrations**: Meta (Facebook/Instagram), TikTok, Pinterest, Google Ads
  - **Access path**: Klaviyo > Integrations > Explore Apps > Search platform name > Install
  - Help documentation available for each integration setup

- Strategic channel selection

  - **Key principle**: Don’t spread thin across every platform
  - **Selection criteria**: Focus where customers spend most time + historical performance
  - **ROAS analysis**: Review last BFCM and Q4 return on ad spend data
  - **Formula**: (Ad Revenue ÷ Ad Spend) × 100
  - Double down on channels with proven performance

- Lookalike audience creation process

  - **Concept**: Platforms find thousands more customers who resemble your best existing customers
  - **Setup**: Sync best customer lists from Klaviyo, platform algorithms handle targeting
  - **Advantage**: More precise targeting vs. casting wide nets

- **Strategic audience segments for lookalikes**

  - **VIP Segment**:
    - Definition: High number of orders within last 30 days
    - Use case: Introduce brand and highlight flagship products to similar prospects
    - Targeting: Meta identifies profiles similar to existing VIPs
  - **Recent High-Value Purchasers**:
    - Definition: Revenue above specified threshold within last 30 days
    - Benefit: Targets engaged buyers with strong purchase intent
    - Focus: Quality over quantity customer acquisition

- Lead magnet development requirements

  - **Core principle**: Must be compelling enough to trade contact information
  - **Effective options**:
    - Free consultation or trial access
    - Original content (guides, templates, reports)
    - Free gift with purchase
    - **Quiz/questionnaire promising customized solutions** (highly effective for personalization)
  - **Value communication**: Clearly highlight benefits to potential customers

- **Lead ad welcome flow structure (3-5 messages)**

  - **Trigger setup**: “Filled Out Lead Ad” event automatically tracked by Klaviyo
  - **Message 1**: Immediately deliver promised offer from lead magnet
  - **Message 2**: Welcome to brand community, share mission/values, differentiation
  - **Message 3+**: Social proof (top products, customer reviews), loyalty program perks
  - **Personalization advantage**: Use lookalike audience insights to customize flow content
  - **Alternative setup**: Use splits within existing welcome series vs. separate flow

- Lookalike audience implementation checklist

  - Determine most impactful channels for customers and brand
  - Set up chosen platform integrations (Google, Meta, Pinterest, TikTok)
  - Create strategic lookalike audiences for targeted advertising
  - Design compelling lead magnets with clear value propositions
  - Build dedicated lead ad welcome series flows
  - **Workshop recommendation**: “Grow Your List Using Klaviyo’s Meta Integration” for advanced guidance

### Using Existing Customers to Power Acquisition

- **Social proof impact metrics**

  - **Key statistic**: 270% increase in purchase likelihood with 5+ product reviews
  - **Consumer behavior**: Trust other shoppers’ opinions over brand claims
  - **Psychological principle**: Bandwagon effect (like dance floor - few join first, then everyone follows)

- **Review collection strategy and implementation**

  - **Klaviyo Reviews setup** (Shopify/WooCommerce):
    - Navigate to integrations, install Klaviyo Reviews
    - Use pre-built review request flows from Flows library
    - **Standard trigger**: “Fulfilled Order” event
    - **Advanced trigger**: “Ready for Review” (Klaviyo Reviews specific)
  - **Third-party alternatives**: Check ecommerce platform native features or explore App Marketplace
    - Examples: Okendo, [Reviews.io](http://Reviews.io), other review platforms
  - **Timing optimization**: Wait 7 days after product delivery for maximum response rates
  - **Incentivization strategy**: Consider offering rewards for reviews starting 1-2 months before BFCM

- **Multi-channel review utilization strategy**

  - **Website placement**: Product descriptions, dedicated review sections
  - **Email/SMS campaigns**: Feature best reviews in regular communications
  - **Facebook Ads example**: Leonisa approach - include video + unedited customer quote as ad copy
    - **Best practice**: Resist urge to “clean up” language - maintain authentic social media feel
  - **Google Seller Ratings**: Extension pulls star ratings from trusted sources for search results
  - **Signup forms**: New static review block feature
    - Fully customizable: fonts, star styles, text size, colors
    - **Example**: Beantown Coffee highlighting customer review for popular coffee blend

- **User Generated Content (UGC) strategy**

  - **Impact statistic**: 75% of consumers prefer customer photos/videos over professional imagery
  - **Implementation approach**:
    - Choose social platforms where customers are most active
    - Create recognizable branded hashtag for content aggregation
    - **Benefit**: Customers’ friends/followers become free lookalike audiences
  - **Success example**: Sticker U case study
    - Built 28,000+ Instagram followers through customer content encouragement
    - Campaigns: “As Seen on IG,” “CEO Selects”
    - Strategy: Don’t just collect UGC - actively use in marketing campaigns

- **Loyalty and referral program integration**

  - **Note**: Klaviyo doesn’t have native referral function - requires third-party integrations
  - **Access**: Klaviyo Integrations page > explore referral program options
  - **Loyalty program optimization**:
    - Require email opt-in for participation
    - Reward SMS and push notification opt-ins
    - Incentivize friend referrals for list growth
  - **Referral program tactics**:
    - Promote in all post-purchase communications
    - **BFCM strategy**: Consider increasing referral incentives when list growth is top priority

- **Social proof implementation checklist**

  - Explore review, UGC, loyalty, and referral integrations in Klaviyo directory
  - Select methods best fitting brand and customer behavior
  - **Execution priorities**:
    - Ask existing customers for reviews via automated flows
    - Showcase reviews across all channels (website, emails, ads)
    - Encourage UGC sharing with strategic hashtag campaigns
    - Amplify customer content across marketing channels
    - Leverage loyalty/referral programs to turn customers into brand advocates
