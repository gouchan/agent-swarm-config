### Sports Betting Fundamentals

- Market dynamics: Sharp vs recreational sportsbooks
  - Sharp books take bets from anyone, including pros
  - Recreational books (TV advertised) avoid sharp bettors
  - Sharp books set “true” market prices, recreational books follow
- Understanding odds and vig
  - \-110 on both sides = 52.4% break-even rate (due to sportsbook commission)
  - Vig varies by book - shop for lowest margins
  - Key numbers in NFL: 3 and 7 (most common margins)
  - Half points extremely important around key numbers

### Finding Edges and Value

- Top-down betting strategy (easiest for beginners)
  - Compare sharp sportsbook lines to recreational book prices
  - Use odds screens to spot discrepancies quickly
  - Speed crucial - markets move fast
- Bottom-up betting (handicapping)
  - Create own projections using models/analysis
  - Much harder than top-down approach
  - Most recreational bettors fail at this method
- Closing line value as edge measurement
  - Getting better price than market closing line indicates edge
  - Essential metric for long-term success

### Advanced Strategies and Markets

- Live betting opportunities
  - Watch for injuries, weather changes not reflected in lines
  - Act during commercial breaks to avoid broadcast delays
- Player props often most exploitable
  - Lower limits = less confident pricing
  - Bet “unders” and “no” outcomes (85-90% of profits historically)
  - Public biased toward things happening vs not happening
- Futures betting
  - Beatable due to infrequent updates by sportsbooks
  - Don’t tie up money long-term unless significant edge

### Bankroll and Risk Management

- Never bet emotional - remove personal bias completely
- Flat staking: 1% of bankroll per bet (conservative approach)
- Track all bets in spreadsheet for pattern analysis
- Set emotional bankroll limit (point where losses become painful)
- Account priming techniques to avoid limits
  - Act like losing bettor initially
  - Use bonuses suboptimally, make parlays, bet close to game time
  - Avoid referral codes from sharp bettors

### Psychological and Behavioral Factors

- Process over outcomes mentality essential
- Expect 46% loss rate even with edge
- Never chase losses with larger bets
- Remove fandom - former players aren’t good bettors despite knowledge
- Scientific method approach: observe, hypothesize, test, verify

### Technology and Future Outlook

- Odds screens and calculators eliminate manual math
- AI will impact both sides (bettors and sportsbooks)
- New markets and states create ongoing opportunities
- Limiting by sportsbooks major challenge for successful bettors
- Partnership networks needed to maintain betting access
