When the presenter mentions “100 to 10k units sold on TikTok Shop,” he’s referring to the process and strategies for growing your product sales on the platform—from selling your first 100 units to reaching 10,000 units or more.

What Does This Mean for You? 





Early Stage (100 units): This is the beginning phase, where you’re just starting out. You might be testing your product, figuring out what content works, and learning how to drive your first sales.



Growth Stage (1,000+ units): As you get traction, you’ll want to increase your video output (video volume), improve your content, and possibly use paid ads or influencer partnerships to reach more people.



Scale Stage (10,000 units): At this level, you’re optimizing everything—your shop, your videos, your marketing, and your fulfillment. You’re likely using analytics to double down on what works and may even automate parts of your process.

Why Is This Important? 





It’s about scaling: The presenter is showing that it’s possible to start small and, with the right approach, grow to significant sales numbers.



GMV (Gross Merchandise Value): As your units sold increase, your GMV (total sales value) goes up. This is a key metric for success on TikTok Shop.



Video Volume: The more (and better) videos you post, the more likely you are to reach these higher sales numbers.

Do You Need a Seller Account? 

Yes, you need a TikTok Shop seller account to list products and track your sales as you scale from 100 to 10,000 units.

How to Take Advantage 





Start with a seller account: Register and get approved.



List your products: Add your items to your TikTok Shop.



Create lots of videos: Use creative, engaging content to promote your products.



Track your sales: Use TikTok Shop analytics to see what’s working.



Scale up: As you find what works, increase your video output and marketing efforts to grow your sales volume.

Why Would a Shop Give You Free Stuff? 





Product Seeding: Shops want their products promoted by creators and affiliates who can drive sales. Sending free samples is a way to get their products featured in videos, reviews, and live streams.



Affiliate Marketing: If you’re an affiliate, you earn a commission for every sale you help generate. Shops give you products so you can create content and encourage your followers to buy.

What Do Shops Want to See in an Affiliate Account? 

Shops want to work with affiliates who can actually help them sell. Here’s what they look for:





Active, Engaged Audience:





Followers who watch, like, and comment on your content.



Engagement rate is often more important than just follower count.



Relevant Content:





Your videos should match the shop’s target market (e.g., beauty, fashion, tech).



Shops want to see you’ve promoted similar products successfully.



Consistent Posting:





Regular, high-quality content shows you’re serious and can keep their product visible.



Sales History (if any):





If you’ve driven sales for other brands, mention it! Shops love affiliates with a proven track record.



Professional Profile:





Clear bio, contact info, and links to your TikTok Shop or affiliate page.

Apply to TikTok Shop Affiliate Program: You can join as an affiliate and connect with brands looking for promoters.



Reach Out to Shops Directly: Message shops and offer to create content in exchange for free products.



Showcase Your Value: Share your stats (followers, engagement, past sales) and explain how you’ll promote their products.

What Shops Expect in Return 





Content Creation: Unboxing, reviews, tutorials, or live streams featuring their products.



Sales: Use your affiliate link so they can track sales and pay you commission.

### TikTok Shop Business Model Fundamentals

-   **What is TikTok Shop**: E-commerce platform integrated into TikTok app
    -   Two-click purchase system (no external links required)
    -   Buy products directly within TikTok while scrolling
-   **Affiliate System**: Earn commissions promoting products from massive brands
    -   Don’t need to own products or hold inventory
    -   Brands handle shipping and customer service
    -   You create content, earn percentage of sales

    -   Get paid every 7 days directly from TikTok

    -   Niche doesn’t matter - TikTok algorithm targets right audiences

### Account Requirements and Acquisition

-   **Three Methods to Get Qualified Account**:

        -   Free organic growth guide provided at end of training

### Bottom of Funnel (BOF) Video Creation Method

-   **Core Concept**: Target people already interested in product (don’t need to sell benefits, just price/deal)
    
-   **Seven-Step BOF Formula**:
    
    1.  **Open Loop/Pattern Interrupt**: “What if I said one lime for free?”
    2.  **Transition**: “Don’t believe me? Tap that orange shopping cart”
    3.  **Why Should They Do It**: “To unlock that flash sale”
    4.  **Value**: “Use code LIME50 at checkout for 50% off”
    5.  **Close the Loop**: “Making one essentially free with buy-one-get-one”
    6.  **Second CTA**: “But don’t wait, tap before deal ends tonight”
    7.  **Complete Example**: Combines all steps into 30-second video script
-   **Faceless Method** (Easiest Approach):
    
    -   Film product for 30 seconds with decent lighting, vertical format
    -   Search “Legit Tim” on TikTok for broad-use audio clips
    -   Use trending sounds like “What do you mean the sale ends today?”
    -   Add text overlay: “Last day of sale” + specific discount percentage
    -   Point to orange shopping cart
    -   **Volume Target**: 30-60 videos per day
    -   **Reset Rule**: If videos consistently get under 200 views, stop posting for 24 hours
-   **Success Metrics**: Focus on click-through rate (3.72% example shown)
    
    -   Algorithm rewards high-click videos with more views
    -   More views lead to more sales opportunities
